What this is

Mise en Scroll is a side project I built for myself: a single place to browse new posts from the food blogs I actually follow, without jumping between tabs or relying on social media algorithms to surface them.

Every card links back to the original post. I don't host any recipes. The site keeps each post's title, date, thumbnail, a short excerpt and its ingredient list (so you can search by ingredient), all read from the blog's public feed, sitemap or recipe page. Photos are linked from the original site, and a recipe's steps are read live from the original page when you open a card. They're never stored here.

A note on content ownership

All recipes, photos, and writing belong to their original authors. I'm not claiming any of it. This is just a reading tool, the same way a podcast app doesn't own the podcasts.

Feeds and sitemaps are public so readers and search tools can find a site's posts. That's all this uses them for.

Want your blog removed?

Open a GitHub issue or email mise.en.scroll.app@gmail.com and I'll take it down within 48 hours.

No ads, no tracking

It's a personal project with no advertising, affiliate links, analytics or monetisation.
